Reboot Workshop node?

It will be offline for about 10 seconds.

Remove env0?

env0 (BME280) stops reporting and its pins (SDA 21, SCL 22) are freed. You can add it again later.

Delete rule r32?

Rule r32 stops running on the device. Its pins keep their current settings.
