# BME280 (Hardware)

Firmware: [docs/features/bme280.md](../../docs/features/bme280.md) · Node-RED: [nodered/.../docs/bme280.md](../../nodered/node-red-contrib-de-esp32/docs/bme280.md) · Manager: [manager/docs/bme280.md](../../manager/docs/bme280.md)

## What it needs from the hardware
An I2C bus (default `i2c0`: SDA 21, SCL 22) at 100 kHz, address 0x76 or 0x77, up to 2 sensors, 3.3 V.

## Provided today
Nothing yet for the sensor. The expansion header (`hardware/lib/expansion-header.tsx`) carries SDA, SCL and 3.3 V, but no design places a BME280.

## Electrical limits
The ESP32's internal pull-ups are weak, so use 4.7 kΩ pull-ups on SDA/SCL (breakouts usually fit them). `relay-8` fits its own 4.7k pull-ups and says to leave them off (DNP) if the base board has them, so check before adding another set.

## Gaps
The ESP32-S3 base board is planned but not designed; no `hardware/designs/` entry exists. The firmware currently targets the classic ESP32, so pin numbers here are classic-ESP32 pins. A BME280 footprint or connector, and the base board's I2C pins and pull-ups, are not designed.
